# The foreign member reads 0.923, which is the number its author says it is

2026-08-28. What one board slot bought, and why it changed the plan more than a better score would have.

## The measurement

`kneemri-raptor` served `raptor_ft_coatnet_v4_full_swa` ALONE — our kit, our decode, our windowing, one
seat, no member of ours in the blend — and the board returned **0.923** (submission 55829913, 00:46Z).

Our own best single, `calibsolo`, reads **0.923** on the same board.

## What it settles

**Our serving of a foreign checkpoint is faithful, and this is the first independent check of it.** On
2026-08-27 the dataset's author wrote (forum topic 737696) that the published weights are "only my 0.924
set". We read 0.923 through a decode path that is entirely ours. A 0.001 agreement means the suspicion
that we were mis-serving somebody else's model — the suspicion that a +0.030 gap was sitting inside our
own preprocessing — had nothing behind it. There was no bug to find.

**The premise of knee-xuj5 needs restating rather than dropping.** "Our member is 0.028 behind the
field's" is true of the field's BEST member and false of the field's PUBLIC one. The public member is
0.924; ours is 0.923; the 0.936-0.938 single the same author says he holds is unreleased, un-blended,
and in the architecture family whose 0.924 sibling we already have on disk.

**And it makes distillation a live axis again, for a stated mechanism.** `distill_pairs` read +0.0016
with a seat value of −0.0003, and the ledger named the cause instead of guessing: at 0.9159 nearest, its
student sat closer to a seated member than any seated member sits to its own nearest neighbour, so "a
rank average cannot pay for an ordering it already holds." That is a verdict on a SELF-teacher. The
board has now handed us a teacher that is equal in quality by the deciding ruler and foreign in
architecture, decode and training corpus — the precise defect that diagnosis named, removed. The arm is
`experiments/arms/raptor_distill.sh` and its pre-registration is in its header.

## What it does not settle

The 0.923 is one number from one serving, and it says nothing about whether the foreign member is worth
a SEAT — a member's board score and its marginal contribution to a blend are different quantities, and
this project has priced members that were distant and negative (`margin_calib`) and members that were
strong and inert (`dark6`'s seventh seat). `kneemri-raptorseat` (55831424) is the entry that asks that
question, and at the time of writing it is still scoring.

Nor does it license reading the author's 0.936-0.938 as a number we could reach by trying harder. It is
a claim about a checkpoint nobody outside his account has run, made in a thread, with no OOF alongside
it. What it justifies is a WATCH (knee-zpgr) — one dataset-list poll a session, no card, no slot —
because the measured ensemble bonus is +0.0143 (knee-urvf, three clean pairings) and a 0.936 member
seated in our six predicts 0.950 from an asset the author has said he intends to publish.

## The teacher pass, and one number worth keeping

Scoring all 4,407 train studies through the foreign member took **35 minutes across three processes**
and would have taken 2.3 hours in one. The card measured **0% utilisation** during the serial attempt:
the pass is DICOM-decode bound, not compute bound, and every future pass of a foreign member over our
corpus should be sharded on that basis (`--shard/--shards`, joined by `--merge`). Zero studies failed to
decode, so the teacher file has no fallback rows in it.

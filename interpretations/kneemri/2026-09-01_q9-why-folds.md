# Q9 (knee-b1s8): why folds at all when a scored public test exists

2026-09-01, Kimi (researcher/auditor session). Every number below is read off disk; the constructor
citations are code, not prose.

## What the scheme IS, read out of the code

- `core/data/corpus/folds.py:39,59-60` — five folds, assigned by `sha1("2026:" + report_text) % 5`.
  The hash is of the REPORT TEXT so duplicate-report groups never straddle the divide; the leak that
  removes is measured in the module docstring: 177 of 4,407 studies sit in a duplicate group, the
  largest 37 studies sharing one template.
- `folds.py:19-33` — a study the train table does not know (a foreign corpus in a merged cache) is
  `TRAIN_ONLY = -2`: it trains under every fold and is never scored.
- `--fold -1` is not a fold. `folds.py:63-67`: `holdout(assignments, -1)` selects `assignments == -1`,
  and no study is ever assigned -1, so the holdout is **empty by construction** and training is every
  study, foreign included. The run records NaN rather than omitting the key
  (`kneemri/experiments/trajectory_check.py:162`), and no `HoldoutRecord` is ever written — which is
  the disk-level fact every downstream refusal keys on (`kneemri/runs/holdout_record.py`,
  `kneemri/kaggle/seat_value.py:114-127`).
- Sizes: the merged cache holds 5,143 studies; fold 0 splits 4,234 train / 909 holdout
  (`experiments/README.md:3638`). `docs/PLAN.md:58` still says "one fold of 881 studies" — 881 is
  4,407/5, the equal-split figure before report-hash grouping and the merged cache; the fold-0 holdout
  every recent number is measured on is **909**. Stale constant, worth a line's fix.

## What nobody decided

There is no recorded decision choosing five folds, or fold 0 as the standard holdout, anywhere in the
ledger, `interpretations/`, `docs/`, or the splitting code (full-text audit, 132 fold mentions in
`experiments/README.md`). `folds: int = 5` is a bare default (`folds.py:39`); the ledger's fold-0
convention simply appears in its header (`experiments/README.md:271`). Folds 1–4 were trained once for
the original five-fold ensemble (`member_fold0..4`, `experiments/README.md:732-736`) and abandoned —
fold 0 survived by inheritance, not by selection. The one split decision that IS on record is that the
split is a property of the corpus, not the run (`kneemri/training/train.py:72-77` — deriving it from
`--seed` once gave seed 7 a holdout sharing only 192 of 909 studies with seed 2026's).

So Claude's suspicion is confirmed as far as the CHOICES go: 5 and 0 are historical. The scheme around
them is not: the report-hash grouping, the corpus-fixed split seed, and the -1/-2 sentinels are each
reasoned and measured.

## Why fold 0 earns its place — what the scored public test cannot do

The public test set is the better ruler and the expensive one. Five slots a day, reset 00:00 UTC, a
spent slot is a permanent fact on a public board, and the owner paces them uniformly across the day.
Fold 0 is free, instant, and paired. What it buys that a submission cannot:

- **Paired resolution.** Two recipes on the same 909 studies compare at roughly half the unpaired
  Hanley–McNeil floor (0.0053 unpaired; `docs/PLAN.md:58-62`). A board slot resolves about 0.001 with
  no pairing at all.
- **Ordering without spending.** The holdout orders the field: 0 inversions over 108 resolved pairs
  inside the fold-0 family (`interpretations/kneemri/2026-08-25_the-all-data-boundary.md`); the
  offline-maximum principle's track record is in `comp/champion.json` ("THE OFFLINE MAXIMUM IS A
  SUPPORTED MOVE").
- **Selection without touching the board.** Sixty-five fold-0 records sharing 909 studies price seats
  card-free (`experiments/README.md:379`). No board slot can do that at any price.

What the public set buys that fold 0 cannot: it is the only ruler reading the real quantity — AUC
against radiologist-annotated evaluation studies, where every local number is agreement with
LLM-read report labels (`docs/PLAN.md:45-51`: 58 of 4,407 studies carry the twelve annotations, and
the dozen in fold 0's holdout sit in the ±0.17 regime). The proxy compresses about 2x
(`board_transfer`, currently +1.92 over 15 entries — read the tool, not this prose). Division of
labour, stated in PLAN and confirmed by everything since: **the fold decides which recipe to submit;
the board decides whether it was better.**

## Why fold -1 earns its place — and exactly what it costs

The case: once a recipe is fixed, the holdout is 909 studies (20.6% of 4,407) the shipped model never
trains on. The measured dose, board ruler, pre-registered before the read
(`interpretations/kneemri/2026-08-24_the_all_data_dose_response.md`):

| all-data seats | entry | board | per seat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | gradeswap / accumseat / greedy7 | 0.933 | — |
| 1 | finalfit_v1 | 0.934 | +0.001 |
| 6 | allfit_v1 | 0.937 | +0.0006 |

Real and sub-linear: the first seat buys about +0.001, the next five +0.003 together — six all-data
members largely re-learn the same 909 studies. The matched board twin says the same thing at one seat:
`lastseat_v1` (fold-0 `small_batch6_s2026`) against `dark7_v1` (the same member refit at `--fold -1`)
read **0.937 both ways** — one flag moved, board identical
(`interpretations/kneemri/2026-08-25_the-all-data-boundary.md`).

The cost, and it is the finding's other half: a fold -1 member is **unscoreable forever**
(`comp/champion.json`: "A MEMBER FITTED AT --fold -1 IS UNSCOREABLE FOREVER"). Not weakly measurable —
no holdout record exists and none can ever be created, because the member has seen every study one
could score it on. The bill has come due three times, each time as a ruler going dark:

1. 2026-08-24 (`seat_value.py:83-94`, knee-1gdf): the pin went to `allfit`, six of seven members
   fold -1, one scoreable seat below the PRICEABLE=2 minimum — every arm that finished after printed a
   traceback where its seat should be. The ruler that decides what ships went dark as a side effect of
   the board improving.
2. `comp/board.jsonl`: 27 rows record `offline: null` with `offline_scope: in-sample`; the pinned
   champion (`raptorweight_v1`, 0.944) is five of six seats fold -1, so the entry that carries the
   board has no offline number of any kind. `champion_uncertainty` refuses it for the same reason
   (`kneemri/kaggle/champion_uncertainty.py:14-20`).
3. The substitution trap, measured (`the-all-data-boundary.md`): scoring a blind entry by its fold-0
   twins produced 0.8687 against peak8's 0.8731 — a confident, wrong answer, fourteen board ticks wide
   on a gap the holdout resolves. Hence the standing rule (`comp/champion.json`): an offline number may
   only be compared against an offline number fitted on the SAME studies; there is no comparison across
   the fold -1 line, with or without a correction.

There is one more quiet cost: distillation teachers must be gated to fold-0 twins, because a fold -1
teacher's reading of a training study is memorised
(`kneemri/training/objective.py:384-390`).

## The ledger's own hygiene, audited

The same-studies rule holds everywhere it matters. Where fold 1/2 members are quoted beside fold-0
rows the delta column is withheld (`experiments/README.md:295,306,732-736`); `wholestack_fold2` is
explicitly declared unmatchable (line 790-791); corpus scans exclude other folds from the fold-0 pool
(line 4868). Two violations exist, both early-era aggregates predating the rule: the fold-0+fold-1 mean
0.8481 compared against the five-fold mean 0.8269 (lines 980-982), and the five-fold mean itself
(754-755) — the second is harmless arithmetic verifying an ensemble figure, the first drew a "+0.021
better each" conclusion that is cross-fold AND recipe-confounded and should be read as void. Board
deltas across the fold -1 line (e.g. "0.937 against 0.943 is the measured dose gap", line 5493) are
cross-boundary but sanctioned: the board is the same hidden test for both entries.

## The decision this answer supports

Keep the scheme; write down the rule that was previously implicit:

- **Fold 0 stays the measurement fold.** Free, paired, fixed by corpus hash, and its ruler is validated
  where it can be (ordering, monotone transfer at 4/5 then 6/7 entry pairs). Five folds and fold 0 are
  convention, but the audit found no cheaper scheme that keeps the paired property, and rotating the
  holdout would invalidate every ledger comparison to date for no measured gain.
- **A member may be fitted blind (`--fold -1`) when, and only when, its recipe is already closed by
  fold-0 measurement AND it is fitted to ship, not to be measured.** The dose is real (+0.001 first
  seat, sub-linear after), and the price is permanent blindness for that member.
- **The pool must never go fully blind.** The ruler needs at least PRICEABLE=2 scoreable champion
  members, and the project's own history says what happens otherwise: the 2026-08-24 traceback era
  ended only when seat reads moved to the greedy fold-0 proxy. A ship candidate of all-data members is
  legitimate; a pool state where no scoreable proxy roster exists is not.
- **No number crosses the fold -1 line.** Not a weak one, not a corrected one — measured, that
  substitution is 14 ticks of confidence in the wrong direction.

What this does NOT do: re-open folds as an axis. The owner's ban stands and nothing here argues with
it — fold ensembling measured flat (`docs/PLAN.md:217`), the five-fold members average to the quoted
ensemble figure, and seeds price the noise floor (nf7 0.8121 / nf13 0.8158). This audit prices what
the existing folds ARE.

## What the morning established

Two pages earlier today: the five-fold ruler resolves at **0.0028** where fold 0 resolves at 0.0062
(sqrt scaling measured, 2.24 against 2.24), it costs **2.1 h** per knob at 224/8, and its one assumption —
that a knob's SIGN survives the change of scale — was checked and holds: **six dual-scale pairs, six sign
agreements, zero flips.** The instrument works.

## What it has left to measure

The rotation's only product is a resolved sign on a delta the fold-0 ruler cannot see. That ships something
**only when the non-default is better than the default.** Resolving a negative leaf re-confirms a value we
already ship and buys nothing.

`knee-dry3h` priced 22 of 70 defaulted leaves with an isolating pair. Of those, by its own words,
*"only two leaves tilt positive at our band"*:

| leaf | step | median | pairs | rotatable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--weight-decay` | 0.02 -> 0.1 | **+0.0029** | 5, 4/5 positive | **no — regulariser** |
| `--warmup` | 0.15 -> 0.05 | **+0.0025** | 5, 4/5 positive | yes |

Every other priced leaf tilts negative: `--spacing` 5/5, `--label-errors` 26/26, `--side-weight` 12/12,
`--contrast-weight` 9/12, `--grad-clip` 8/10, `--slot-pooling` 5/6 (median −0.0055), `--centre` 3/5,
`--mixup` 6/11, `--train-fraction` 24/24.

`--weight-decay` is a **regulariser**, which is the one and only failure class the epoch-ladder lookup
named this morning: the 8-vs-24-epoch baseline is flat *in the absence of slot dropout*, and drop40 x long
interact by +0.0113, so a knob that interacts with regularisation is measured in the flat regime and
deployed in the interacting one. For that class a rotation sign is a hypothesis, not a verdict. It is not a
leaf the instrument can settle.

**So the corpus contains exactly one rotatable positive.**

## Why this is a result and not a dead end

The de-risking step did its job and the answer is more useful than a sign flip would have been. A flip would
have said *the instrument is blind*. What actually happened is that **the instrument works and the target is
empty** — which retires 8-12 h of planned card rather than spending it, and it does so with the transfer
property proven and reusable for any future leaf that does tilt positive.

It also closes the tension flagged this morning without needing a judgement call: the wide-ruler program
*was* knob work dressed at a finer ruler, and `knee-dry3h`'s conclusion — that knob work at the shipping
corner is exhausted in every direction the corpus can price — now applies to it directly. The residual is
downstream of the encoder and the optimiser: **supervision quality or slice-to-study aggregation.**

Both of those have an arm rather than a plan: `dml_a` (mutual supervision, on the card now) and
`slicecover36` (knee-jb0ci, the input-side instrument for aggregation).

## What re-opens the rotation

A leaf that tilts positive above ~0.002 and is not a regulariser. None exists in the 22 priced leaves. The
48 unpriced ones could contain one, but pricing a leaf costs the same 2.1 h as resolving it, so that is a
search, not a lever — `knee-dry3h` consequence (4) already says so and this page does not disagree.

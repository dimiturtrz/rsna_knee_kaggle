## What this table does and does not license

Does: it is the per-band decomposition, so any future question about where a member's macro lives can
be answered without recomputing. It also records that no member in the competent pool is more than
0.0096 away from what its overall macro predicts for the hard bands.

Does not: it does not rank members on hard-band strength. The `resid` column has a measured 95%
half-width of +/-0.0029 per member against a true spread of sd 0.0020 — the measurement is wider than
the thing measured. The two largest residuals in the FULL pool (`focus_latmen_s2026` +0.0837 at overall
0.4747, `focus_mcl_s2026` +0.0632 at 0.5332) are broken members, which is why every headline figure in
D195 is quoted on the competent pool.

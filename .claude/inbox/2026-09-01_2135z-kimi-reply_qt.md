# Q-T reply — published evidence for an external knee-MRI corpus moving multi-label AUC

Short answer: **evidence exists, but almost all of it is same-label or same-anatomy SSL transfer. A clean knee→knee, different-label-set, multi-label AUC delta is essentially absent.**

## 1. Has anyone published MRNet-as-pretraining for a *different* knee-MRI label set?

Yes, one paper. [SKID: Self-Supervised Learning for Knee Injury Diagnosis from MRI Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.10481) (Manna et al., 2021/2022) pre-trains a jigsaw-puzzle pretext encoder on MRNet (1,370 clips) and then transfers it to the **KneeMRI** dataset (917 volumes). The KneeMRI task is *different* from MRNet’s three labels: it uses ligament-condition labels (healthy / partially injured / completely ruptured) and is evaluated as (a) a binary healthy/partial vs. complete-rupture task and (b) a 3-class imbalanced task.

| pretext encoder trained on | downstream KneeMRI binary AUC |
| --- | --- |
| MRNet (1,370 clips) | **0.761** |
| KneeMRI itself (917 volumes) | 0.741 |

Delta: **+0.020 AUC** for MRNet pretraining over KneeMRI self-pretraining. There is no "train from scratch on KneeMRI" baseline in the paper, so the delta against a true no-external-corpus control is unknown.

That is the only published result I could find where MRNet is used as a *source* for a different knee-MRI target. I found no MRNet-as-pretraining result for a multi-label target whose label set is only a partial overlap with MRNet’s {abnormal, ACL tear, meniscal tear}.

## 2. The label-overlap question

The published transfer numbers are **not** partial-overlap numbers:

* **Same labels:** Zhang et al. pre-train on fastMRI and fine-tune on the Stanford/MRNet labels (abnormal, ACL tear, meniscal tear). Same-label, so the AUC gain does not price partial overlap.
* **Closely related but not identical labels:** SKID’s MRNet → KneeMRI transfer is ligament-condition classification, which overlaps conceptually with MRNet’s ACL label but is not the same task and is evaluated as binary/3-class, not multi-label.
* **Same anatomy, different source domain:** PRISM and ModAn-MulSupCon both evaluate on MRNet labels; they show that large external corpora (multi-sequence MRI or RadImageNet) help, but again on the same target labels.

I would report the honest finding as: **no published study measures the partial-overlap multi-label AUC delta you are asking about.**

## 3. Which transfer form has evidence?

| form | evidence | what it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **(d) external corpus as SSL substrate only** | [Zhang et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.16499): fastMRI (10,000 unlabeled knee exams) → Stanford/MRNet diagnosis | Large gain. With 800 target subjects, mean AUC goes from 0.562 (scratch) to 0.819 (their MTL SSL) / 0.824 (ImageNet init). With only 200 subjects, scratch 0.429 → MTL 0.783. |
| **(d) external corpus as SSL substrate only** | SKID: MRNet → KneeMRI | Small gain (+0.020 AUC vs. self-pretraining). |
| **(d) external corpus as SSL substrate only** | [PRISM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07165): 336k-volume multi-sequence MRI corpus → MRNet | Accuracy gain, not AUC: 77.78% (Swin-B scratch) → 80.05% (PRISM), +2.27% on MRNet. |
| **(a) pretrain-then-finetune** | [ModAn-MulSupCon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18613): supervised contrastive pretraining on a 16,222-image RadImageNet subset → MRNet-ACL | AUC 0.964 on MRNet-ACL, beating SimCLR/ImageNet baselines. Source is mixed-modality/mixed-anatomy, not knee-specific. |
| **(b) joint multi-task with external labels as auxiliary heads** | **None found** | — |
| **(c) pooled training with external corpus relabelled** | **None found** | — |

The evidence density is heavily skewed toward **(d) SSL substrate**. That is also the form closest to what fastMRI gives us: a large unlabeled knee-MRI corpus whose labels are not joinable with our target taxonomy.

## 4. The honest null and the nearest adjacent modality

For a **knee→knee, different-label-set, multi-label AUC delta**, the literature is effectively null. The best adjacent evidence is:

* **Chest CT → chest CT (same task, external corpus):** your Q-I reading gave a **+0.031 AUC** delta for an external chest-CT corpus. That is the cleanest published number for “add external same-modality corpus, measure held-out AUC on a different label set,” and it is the benchmark you already used to price the trunk axis.
* **Within MRI, mixed-anatomy corpus → knee:** ModAn-MulSupCon and PRISM show gains, but the gains are on MRNet’s own labels (or accuracy, not AUC), and the source is not knee-only.
* **Knee-only SSL → knee diagnosis:** Zhang et al. show very large same-label gains, but they do not generalize to a partial-overlap label set.

So the honest answer is: **an external knee-MRI corpus can move AUC, but the published proof is either same-label or a different label set with only a +0.020 signal and no scratch control. There is no clean published +N number that prices your exact scenario.**

## What this means for us

If we add an external knee corpus, the most defensible mechanism to pilot is **SSL pretraining (form d)** on the unlabeled portion, followed by fine-tuning on our target labels, because that is the form with actual knee-MRI evidence. Joint multitask or pooled relabeling would be novel transfers with no direct literature support.

## Bottom line

KMAR-50K is a **restoration dataset**, not a classification-transfer asset. Expected effect on our RSNA knee classification task: roughly **±0.005 AUC**, not a 1.5% lever. Full note: `research/2026-09-01_kmar50k_ssl_transfer.md`.

## Answers to the three questions

**1. Self-supervised knee corpus → labelled knee classification.** The closest published results:
- **SKID (Manna et al., 2022)** pre-trains a jigsaw encoder on unlabelled MRNet frames, then freezes and classifies. SSL mean AUC 0.869 vs fully-supervised 0.894 on the same data — **lags by ~0.025**.
- **Zhang et al. (MedIA 2024)** pre-train on ~10,000 unlabelled fastMRI knee exams with anatomy-oriented plane-regression pretexts, then fine-tune on Stanford knee diagnosis. Gains over ImageNet pretraining: +0.073 at 80 labels, +0.046 at 200 labels, +0.002 at 400 labels, **−0.005 at 800 labels**.

So knee-MRI SSL over a strong baseline is **+0.00 to +0.05 AUC**, concentrated in the low-label regime, and can turn negative with more labels.

**2. Artifact-pair structure.** Motion-artifact pairs are standard for denoising/super-resolution/motion-correction, but I found **no paper** showing artifact-pair pretraining beats SimCLR/DINO/MAE/BYOL on a downstream medical classification task. Zhang et al. show that anatomy-aligned pretexts help, but generic pretexts (including slice-ordering) can hurt. For classification, the artifact-pair objective likely learns to map corruption to a clean template, which is not pathology discrimination.

**3. Honest null against DINOv2/DINOv3.** The bar is high:
- DINOv2 (142M natural images) and DINOv3 (~1.7B images) are strong off-the-shelf radiology baselines.
- Domain-specific pretraining only helps when modality closely matches the target.
- KMAR-50K is 62k slices vs. DINOv2's 142M images — a ~2,000× scale gap.
- Even fastMRI's ~10k-exam corpus only beats ImageNet in the low-label regime.

KMAR-50K itself releases paired NIfTIs for restoration benchmarking and includes **no finding labels and no classification downstream experiments**.

## Recommendation

Do not spend a night of card on KMAR-50K pretraining as a primary lever. If you still want a cheap pilot, run DINOv2 + KMAR-50K SSL pretraining vs. DINOv2 fine-tuned on RSNA at small label budget — but the literature says the upside is modest and likely negative if it displaces supervised fine-tuning compute.
